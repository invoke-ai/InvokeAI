The **Layers** panel sits to the right of the Canvas in the **Edit** layout. It has three parts, from top to bottom:

1. **Color** — the color picker and swatches used by the painting tools.
2. **Layers** and **History** — the canvas's layers, or its undo history.
3. **Properties**, **Transform** and **Overview** — the settings of the active tool and of the selected layer.

Drag the dividers between the parts to resize them, and use the chevron at the right of the Color and Properties tabs to collapse them. The sizes are saved with the project.

<Image src={layersPanel} alt="The Layers panel: the color wheel at the top, the layer list grouped into Inpaint Masks, Regional Guidance, Control Layers and Raster Layers, and the Properties pane showing a control layer's Adapter type, Model and Weight." width={420} />

For an introduction to the layer types, see [Crafting images with the Canvas](/users-guide/canvas/introduction/#canvas-concepts).

## Color

The **Color** tab edits the two painting colors: the **Foreground color**, which the brush and text use, and the **Background color**. Click a chip to choose which one you are editing.

* The color wheel, or a box picker via **Switch to box picker**, and a field for the value. The format button cycles through hex, RGB, HSL and HSB.
* **Swap foreground and background colors** (**X**) and **Reset colors to black and white** (**D**).
* **Pick a color from the canvas** — the eyedropper. You can also hold **Alt** on the canvas. A magnifying loupe shows the pixel under the pointer and its RGB values; see the [Canvas guide](/users-guide/canvas/canvas-guide/#tools).
* When an inpaint mask or regional guidance layer is selected, a third chip, **Mask tint**, edits the color of its overlay.

The **Swatches** tab has built-in **Swatches**, your **Recent colors**, and the **Project palette**: click **+** (**Add current color to palette**) to save a color with the project, and right-click a swatch to remove it.

## Layers

### The layer list

Layers are grouped into four stacks: **Inpaint Masks**, **Regional Guidance**, **Control Layers** and **Raster Layers**. Within a stack, higher layers are drawn over lower ones. Click a stack's header to collapse it. Its buttons add a **New layer** of that type and, for masks, regional guidance and control layers, **Hide all** or **Show all**. The Raster Layers header also has **Merge visible** and **Export to PSD**.

Each row shows, from left to right:

* A colored stripe, if you gave the layer a color label.
* A dot that turns the layer on or off (**Enable/disable layer**). A disabled layer takes no part in generation.
* A thumbnail, the layer type and the name.
* An eye button (**Show/hide layer**) on masks, regional guidance and control layers. It only hides the layer from view; a hidden layer still affects generation.
* A lock button (**Toggle lock**), which prevents editing.

Rows can have child rows, shown with a chevron: adjustments and regenerate regions on raster layers, noise and denoise limits on inpaint masks, and reference images on regional guidance layers. Select a child row to edit it in Properties.

Drag an adjustment to reorder it, or drop it on another raster layer or group, or among that layer's adjustments, to move it there as one undoable step. Reference images move between regional guidance layers the same way. A child row's right-click menu also has a **Move to layer** submenu listing the layers that can take it.

### Working with layers

* **Add** a layer with the **+** button next to the Layers and History tabs, or by right-clicking empty space in the list. The new layer is placed above the selected one and selected.
* **Select** a layer by clicking it. **Ctrl**-click adds or removes layers from the selection, and **Shift**-click selects a range.
* **Rename** a layer by double-clicking its name or pressing **F2**. **Enter** saves and **Escape** cancels.
* **Reorder** layers by dragging them, or with **Alt+↑** and **Alt+↓**. Layers can only move within their own stack.
* **Blend mode** and **Opacity**, in the row above the list, apply to the selected layer. There are 16 blend modes, from Normal and Multiply to Color and Luminosity.
* **Group** layers into folders with **Group selected layers**, or **Ctrl+G** on the canvas. **Alt+→** moves a layer into the group above it and **Alt+←** moves it out. A group's dot, eye and lock apply to everything in it.
* **Filter layers** at the bottom of the list shows only the layers whose names match.
* The buttons beside the filter **Duplicate selected layers**, **Group selected layers** and **Delete selected layers**.

## History

The **History** tab lists the canvas's edits, oldest first, with the current step highlighted and undone steps in italics. Click a step to go back or forward to it, or use the **Undo** and **Redo** buttons (**Ctrl+Z**, and **Ctrl+Shift+Z** or **Ctrl+Y**).

<Image src={history} alt="The History tab listing the canvas's recent edits, such as Brush stroke, Regional guidance and Positive prompt." width={420} />

The history keeps the last 64 steps. It lasts only while the project is open in your browser: reloading the page, or starting a new canvas, clears it.

## The layer menu

Right-click a layer, or select it and press **Shift+F10**, for its menu. Right-clicking the canvas opens the same menu for the selected layer.

<Image src={layersContext} alt="The menu of a raster layer, with Transform, Rename layer, Fit to bbox, Add adjustment, Add regenerate region, Filter, Select object, Merge down, Copy to, Convert to, Crop layer to bbox, Save layer to uploads, Disable layer, Lock layer, Color label and Delete layer." width={500} />

The icons at the top **Arrange** the layer (move it to the front, forward, backward or to the back), **Duplicate layer** and **Group layers**. Below them:

* **For every layer:**
  * **Rename layer** and **Fit to bbox** (scale the layer to fit inside the generation frame).
  * **Merge down**.
  * **Copy to** — including **Copy layer to clipboard**, and copies to other layer types.
  * **Crop layer to bbox** and **Save layer to uploads** (save it to the Gallery's Uploads).
  * **Disable layer**, **Lock layer** and **Color label**.
  * **Delete layer**.
* **Raster layers:**
  * **Transform**.
  * **Add adjustment** — Brightness/Contrast, Exposure, Levels, Curves, Saturation, Hue or Invert. Adjustments are non-destructive and appear as child rows.
  * **Add regenerate region** — treats the layer's own shape as an inpaint mask.
  * **Filter**, **Select object** and **Run workflow** — process the layer; see the [Canvas guide](/users-guide/canvas/canvas-guide/#filters-and-object-selection).
  * **Boolean operations** — combine the layer with the one below: intersect, cut out, cut away or exclude.
  * **Convert to** — make it a control layer, inpaint mask or regional guidance layer. Text, shape and gradient layers can also be rasterized into plain pixels.
* **Control layers:** **Transform**, **Filter**, **Select object**, **Run workflow**, **Enable transparent preview**, and **Convert to raster layer**.
* **Inpaint masks:** **Add noise**, **Add denoise limit**, **Extract masked area** (copy the masked part of the image to a new layer), and **Hide layer**.
* **Regional guidance:** **Enable auto-negative**, **Add reference image**, and **Hide layer**.

When several layers are selected, the menu also offers **Merge selected layers**.

## Properties

A heading at the top of the **Properties** tab names what it is editing: **Layer:** or **Group:** followed by the selected layer's or group's name, **Operation:** followed by the operation's name while a Filter or Select Object operation runs, or *No layer selected*. It stays in view as you scroll, so you can always tell which layer the settings below belong to.

Below the heading, the tab shows up to three sections, in this order:

1. The running operation's settings — while a **Filter** or **Select Object** operation is running. Starting either from a layer's menu opens the Layers panel on this tab.
2. **Tool** — the options of the active canvas tool; see the [Canvas guide](/users-guide/canvas/canvas-guide/#tool-options).
3. **Layer** — the settings of the selected layer.

:::tip[Scroll down for the layer's settings]
The **Layer** section comes after the tool options, so on most screens it starts below the visible part of the pane. Scroll the Properties pane down to reach it, or drag the divider above the pane upward to make it taller.
:::

The Layer section is dimmed while results are being staged or a canvas generation is running.

### Raster layer

* **Lock transparency** — paint only where the layer already has pixels, leaving transparent areas transparent, like Photoshop's "Lock transparent pixels". Use it to recolor a shape without spilling paint around it, or to refine a subject painted on a transparent layer without growing its outline. Brushes, shapes and pen input all respect the lock.
* **Filter** — start the Filter operation.

Adjustment child rows each have their own settings, such as **Brightness** and **Contrast**, **Exposure**, **Levels** and **Curves** with a channel selector, **Saturation**, and **Hue**. A regenerate region has a **Fill color** and **Fill style**.

### Control layer

* **Adapter type** — ControlNet, T2I Adapter, Control LoRA, Z-Image ControlNet or Anima ControlNet-LLLite, depending on the model selected in the Generate panel.
* **Model** — the installed control models of that type for the same model family. If the list is empty, install one from the starter models. For Anima, the list holds the LLLite control adapters but not the Inpainting adapter, and each LLLite model can drive only one control layer per generation.
* **Weight** — how strongly the guide is followed (0 to 2; the default is 0.75 for ControlNet).
* **Step range** — the part of the generation, from start to finish, in which the guide applies.
* **Control mode** (ControlNet only) — **Balanced**, **More prompt**, **More control** or **Unbalanced**.
* **Transparent preview** — shows the layer semi-transparently over the image.
* **Filter** — process the layer's image into a guide, such as an edge or depth map.

The control image is the layer's own pixels. To use a picture, copy or convert a raster layer, or drop a gallery image on **Add as Control Layer**.

### Inpaint mask

* **Fill color** and **Fill style** (Solid, Grid, Crosshatch, Diagonal, Horizontal or Vertical) — how the mask is drawn on screen.
* **Invert mask** — swap the masked and unmasked areas.

Its child rows, added from the layer menu, set **Noise** (noise added inside the mask before generating, 25% by default) and **Denoise limit** (the most the masked area may change, 80% by default).

### Regional guidance

* **Positive prompt** and **Negative prompt** — the prompts for this region. Each is saved when you click away from the field. The negative prompt is shown only for models that use one.
* **Auto-Negative** — also uses the region's positive prompt as a negative prompt outside the region.
* **Fill color**, **Fill style** and **Invert mask**, as for inpaint masks.

If the selected model does not support regional guidance, a message says that the layer is skipped during generation.

### Regional reference image

A regional guidance layer's reference image is a child row. Select it to set:

* The image: **Choose image** from the Gallery, drop a gallery image on it, or **Upload** a file.
* **Model** — an IP-Adapter (SD 1.5, SDXL) or FLUX Redux (FLUX.1) model.
* For IP-Adapter, **Method** (Full, Style, Composition, Style (strong) or Style (precise)) and **Weight**. For FLUX Redux, **Image influence**.

## Transform and Overview

The **Transform** tab sets the selected layer's **Position**, **Scale** and **Rotation** as numbers. Each change is a separate undoable step.

The **Overview** tab shows a thumbnail of the whole canvas with the visible area outlined. Click it to move the view.

## Keyboard shortcuts

In the layer list:

| Shortcut | Action |
|---|---|
| **↑** / **↓**, **Home** / **End** | Move between layers |
| **→** / **←** | Expand / collapse, or go to the parent group |
| **Enter** or **Space** | Select |
| **F2** | Rename |
| **Delete** / **Backspace** | Delete the layer |
| **Ctrl+C** | Copy the layer to the clipboard |
| **Alt+↑** / **Alt+↓** | Move the layer up / down |
| **Alt+→** / **Alt+←** | Move into the group above / out of its group |
| **Shift+F10** | Open the layer menu |
| **Ctrl+Z** / **Ctrl+Shift+Z** | Undo / redo |

On macOS, use **Cmd** in place of **Ctrl**. More layer shortcuts, such as duplicate, merge and arrange, work while the canvas has focus; see the [Canvas guide](/users-guide/canvas/canvas-guide/#keyboard-shortcuts).
