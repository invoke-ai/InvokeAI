The **Gallery** holds every image and video you generate or upload. It is organized into **boards**: the always-present **Uncategorized** board, a board for each project, and boards you create yourself. From the Gallery you choose where new results go, find media by its prompt or by what it shows, and move, star, download or delete it.

<Image src={overview} alt="The Gallery with the Concept Art board selected: the board list at the top, the Media and Uploads tabs, the search field, and a grid of images with starred items pinned at the top." width={420} />

## The Gallery at a glance

The Gallery has three parts:

* **The header** shows the selected board after *Gallery /*. Click the board name to hide or show the board list. The gear button opens the [Gallery settings](/users-guide/gallery/gallery-reference/#gallery-settings).
* **The board list** has a search field (**Search or create boards**), a **Filter & sort boards** button, and the boards themselves, grouped under **Boards** and, when turned on, **By Date** and **Archived**. Each group can be collapsed.
* **The media area** shows the contents of the selected board, on two tabs:
  * **Media** — images and videos generated in InvokeAI.
  * **Uploads** — images, videos and audio you have uploaded.

  Above the grid are the **Search items** field, the **Starred only** filter, the sort order (**Newest** or **Oldest**) and the upload button.

When the Gallery is narrow, or docked in a side rail, the board list sits above the media grid. When it is wide, the board list becomes a column on the left. Drag the divider between them to resize the list.

Each board shows its cover image, its name and a count such as **76 | 12**: 76 items on the Media tab and 12 on the Uploads tab. A project's board is marked **Project**. On a multi-user installation, other people's boards show their owner's name.

## Boards

### Creating a board

Type a name into **Search or create boards**. If no board has that name, a **Create board "…"** row appears at the end of the list; click it, or press **Enter**. You can also type the name and click the **+** button next to the **Boards** heading.

<Image src={createBoard} alt="Typing Seascapes into the board search field shows a Create board &quot;Seascapes&quot; row." width={420} />

The new board is selected straight away, so it also becomes the destination for new results. A board's cover is chosen automatically: its newest starred item, or its newest item if nothing is starred.

### Choosing where new media goes

**By default, new results go to the board that is selected in the Gallery**: select a board, press **Invoke**, and the results land on that board. The destination is fixed when you press Invoke, so you can select another board and queue more work while earlier jobs are still running, without redirecting them.

* A new project starts with its own board selected. See [Projects](/users-guide/projects/introduction/#where-your-images-go).
* If you select a date board (see below), results go to the project's board, because date boards are only a view.
* Uploads go to the selected board.
* If you archive or delete the selected board, the selection, and with it the destination, moves to **Uncategorized**.

To keep sending results to one board while you browse others, make it the **auto-add board**: choose **Auto-add to this board** from its menu. The board is marked **Auto**, and new generations, Canvas saves, pasted media and the results of workflow nodes whose **Board** is set to **Auto** all go there, whichever board you are viewing. Choose **Stop auto-adding here** from the same menu to go back to following the selected board. The setting behind this is **Send new results to the selected board**, in the [Gallery settings](/users-guide/gallery/gallery-reference/#gallery-settings).

### Finding, filtering and sorting boards

Typing in **Search or create boards** narrows the list to boards whose names contain the text. Selecting a board clears the search.

The **Filter & sort boards** button beside the search field controls which boards are listed and in what order:

<Image src={boardFilters} alt="The Filter & sort boards menu with Board visibility options (Date boards, Archived boards, Other projects' boards) and Sort by options." width={420} />

* **Board visibility**
  * **Date boards** — adds a **By Date** group with one board per day on which you created media. Date boards are read-only views: you cannot upload to them, drop media on them, or rename or delete them.
  * **Archived boards** — adds an **Archived** group listing the boards you have archived.
  * **Other projects' boards** — shows the boards of your other projects, which are hidden by default to keep the list focused. The current project's board is always shown.
* **Sort by** — **Created** (the default) or **Name**, in descending or ascending order. **Uncategorized** and the current project's board always stay at the top.

These choices are saved with the project, so each project can list boards differently.

### Renaming, archiving, downloading and deleting boards

Right-click a board, or hover over it and click its **⋯** button, to rename, archive, download or delete it. [Managing boards](/users-guide/gallery/gallery-reference/#managing-boards) in the Gallery Reference describes each action, including what deleting a board does to its media.

## Finding media in a board

Search, the starred filter and the sort order apply to the selected board and the current tab (Media or Uploads).

### Searching by metadata

Type into **Search items** to find media whose generation metadata contains the text, such as a word from the prompt, a model name or a seed. The search is not case-sensitive and matches the text exactly as typed, spaces included. Media without metadata, such as most uploads, can only be found by date.

To limit results by date, add a date token. The **?** button in the search field shows the syntax:

<Image src={searchHelp} alt="The Search syntax help, listing the from:, to: and date: tokens with examples." width={420} />

* `from:` — on or after a day, e.g. `from:2026-07-14`
* `to:` — on or before a day, e.g. `to:yesterday`
* `date:` — a single day, e.g. `date:today`

Days can be written as `YYYY-MM-DD`, `today`, `yesterday`, or relative to today: `7d`, `2w` and `1m` mean 7 days, 2 weeks and 1 month ago. For example, `lighthouse from:2w` finds media from the last two weeks whose metadata mentions a lighthouse. A token that cannot be read is shown in red.

### Searching by content

When image indexing is turned on, the Gallery can also search by what media *shows*, whatever the prompt said: find "sunset on the beach" even in images that were never prompted with those words. This **semantic search** uses the same index as the [Image Map](/users-guide/image-map/introduction/). To turn indexing on, install the `DFN2B-CLIP-ViT-L-14-39B` model from the starter models and restart the backend; see [Activating the Image Map](/users-guide/image-map/introduction/#activating-the-image-map). When indexing is turned off, the semantic search button is not shown.

<Image src={semanticSearch} alt="A semantic search for &quot;a wizard holding a torch&quot;, with the most similar images first." width={420} />

* **Search by text.** Click the sparkle button at the right end of the search field (**Search semantically**). The field changes to **Describe what to find…**; describe what you are looking for. Any text already in the field is used straight away.
* **Search by image.** Drag an image onto the search field: a thumbnail from the Gallery, an image from the Preview, an image file from your computer, or an image from another browser tab. The Gallery then shows the media most similar to it, labelled **Similar to …**.

Results are ranked by similarity, most similar first, up to 500 items from the selected board. Videos are included. The Media and Uploads tabs, the sort order, the starred filter and date tokens do not apply to ranked results.

To leave semantic search, click the **✕** in the search field, click the sparkle button again (**Exit semantic search**), or switch tabs. If the index model is not installed, the Gallery says so when you try to search. Archived boards are not indexed.

### Sorting and the starred filter

The sort menu orders media by creation time, **Newest** first (the default) or **Oldest** first. The star button next to it (**Starred only**) shows only starred items. Images and videos are always shown together.

### Browsing large boards

The media grid continues through the full listing as you scroll, including older items beyond the first few screens. You can also drag the scrollbar to jump to a distant part of the list; the Gallery loads items near that position without first loading every item in between. Scroll back up to browse earlier positions.

While a part of the list loads, its tile positions stay in place. If one page cannot be loaded, the Gallery marks its first empty tile with an error and **Retry**, while keeping nearby loaded items available.

## Starring favorites

Star an item to keep it at hand. Hover over a thumbnail and click the star in its corner, choose **Star image** (or **Star video**) from its menu, or select items and press **.** (period). Starred thumbnails keep a yellow star.

Starred items are gathered in a **Starred** section at the top of the board, above the rest of the media, which can be collapsed. If there are more than fit, **Show all** switches on the **Starred only** filter. Starring also affects the board's cover, which is its newest starred item.

## Uploading media

Click the upload button at the right of the tab bar (**Upload media to …**) and choose files, or drag files from your computer onto the media grid. An empty board also shows **Drop media here or click to upload**. Uploads go to the selected board, on its **Uploads** tab. You cannot upload to a date board.

You can upload:

* **Images** — PNG, JPEG and WebP.
* **Videos** — stored and played as H.264/AAC MP4. H.264 video in another container, such as an iPhone `.mov` recorded as "Most Compatible", is repackaged almost instantly. Other formats, such as HEVC, ProRes, VP9 and `.wmv`, are converted, which takes roughly as long as the clip on a typical CPU. 10-bit HDR footage is converted to 8-bit without tone mapping, so it will look flatter than the original.
* **Audio** — MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG and similar files become a video that shows the waveform. It behaves like any other video, for example as the soundtrack for [audio-to-video](/users-guide/video-generation/introduction/) generation.

You can also paste an image or video from the clipboard while no text field has focus. A dialog asks whether to upload it to the Gallery, add it to the Canvas, or use it as a reference image.

## Selecting, moving and deleting media

Click an item to select it, and double-click it (or press **Enter** while its thumbnail has keyboard focus) to open it in the Preview, in the center of the workbench. **Shift**-click selects a range, and **Ctrl**-click (**Cmd**-click on macOS) adds or removes single items. **Ctrl+A** selects everything loaded on the page, and **Escape** clears the selection.

While anything is selected, a bar at the bottom of the Gallery shows how many items are selected, with buttons to star them, move them to another board, download them, or delete them.

<Image src={selection} alt="Three selected images with the selection bar at the bottom of the Gallery showing 3 selected." width={420} />

### Moving media to another board

Drag items onto a board in the board list; if the list is hidden, it opens while you drag. You can also use **Change Board** in an item's menu, or the move button in the selection bar. Moving never copies: each item is on one board at a time. **Remove from Board** in the **Change Board** menu moves an item to **Uncategorized**.

### Deleting media

Press **Delete** (or **Backspace**), choose **Delete Image**, **Delete Video** or **Delete Selection** from the menu, or use the delete button in the selection bar. Deleting permanently removes the items from every board and from disk, and cannot be undone. A confirmation asks first. Tick **Don't ask me again** and confirm to stop asking (cancelling leaves it on); you can turn it back on, or off, with **Confirm image deletion** in Settings under Gallery.

Deleting does not check whether an image is still used elsewhere, for example on a canvas or as a reference image, so check before you delete media you are still working with. If deleting is interrupted, InvokeAI cleans up any leftover files the next time it starts.
